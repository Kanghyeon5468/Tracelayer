# Internal Fraud Investigation Policy

## High-Risk Wire Transfers

Wire transfers above 10000 USD to a new country require enhanced review when the customer has no recent travel indicator or matching historical pattern.

## Shared Device or IP

If two or more unrelated customers use the same device or IP address within a short time window, the investigator must review account-opening history and transaction links.

## Human Approval

The system may recommend account hold, outbound transfer block, or enhanced due diligence. It must not execute final asset freeze or customer offboarding without human approval.

## PII Handling

Generated summaries should redact full names, raw email addresses, and direct identifiers unless the viewer has a valid investigation role.
